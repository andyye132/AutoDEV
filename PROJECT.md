# AutoDev — Project Book

A living map of the project: what we claim, what is proven, where breadth and accuracy are thin, what "useful" has to mean, and what we do next. Updated by the agent as results land; the ledger (`LOG.md`) is the audit trail, this is the plan.

*Updated 29 Aug 2026 (Sat evening). Deadline: Sep 5 (OpenReview; abstract registered).*

## Chapter 1 — Thesis and claims

**Thesis.** An autonomous research loop can discover *exploration procedures* — programs over a save/restore archive of simulator states — that reach target states faster than hand-designed exploration and learned intrinsic motivation, and the discovered procedures generalize: to games they never saw and to a class of targets they were never scored on.

| Claim | Status | Evidence | What would strengthen it |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 The loop discovers a procedure better than every hand-designed and RL tester at equal budget | **negative as stated; survives as a cost statement** | fixed harness: evolved 0.579 ≈ action-repeat 0.574; pair-ladder 0.600 (+0.025). K-Goal: at equal budget the procedure ties the best PPO tester (59% vs 56%); PPO at 4× budget overtakes it (72% / 86%); the procedure reaches PPO's 1× detection with 1/4 of the steps and no training | a second suite (K-Goal), a second engine (Craftax) |
| C2 It transfers to unseen levels | **invalid → re-testing** | racy-harness numbers withdrawn; fixed harness at 4× budget: 59% of held-out-level targets (action-repeat 60% at 1×); D1 pending | more held-out levels (generated levels as a held-out set), per-level analysis |
| C3 It transfers to an unseen target class | **invalid → re-testing; cross-engine negative** | racy-harness 47% vs 30% withdrawn (4× fixed: 33%); off-Kinetix the frozen program is mid-pack: random wins Craftax/DoorKey/Unlock, macrosweep wins FourRooms, archive family wins MountainCar/Acrobot | the rotated split (run 3 candidates, evaluating) as a second class |
| C4 The gain is structural, not tuning | **shown** | constants: +0.015 of +0.13; fixed portfolios lose | scaffold comparison (agentic loop, tree search) at equal budget |
| C5 The procedures are *useful* | **thin but sound** | 5 distinct replay-verified anomalies of severity A/B on 138 levels (h15_thrustshoot sustained escape = A), every chain reproducible from reset | solve PPO-unsolved Kinetix levels (K-Goal); Craftax achievements; cost per target |
| C6 ≥ 90% of reachable targets in a single run | **withdrawn** | the 88% / 94% / 74% were racy-harness numbers; recompute from D1 and T2 on the fixed harness with the exclusion policy | pre-registered long-budget reachable set (T2, queued); cleaner search signal (run 5/6); restarts; target-directed designs |

**Rules we hold ourselves to.** Held-out splits sealed before search; reachability defined by a long-budget union computed once; every counted bug replay-verified from a complete recorded chain (append-only lineage log, added after the replay caught two lineage defects); every number regenerated from logs; corrections disclosed (PhyFu novelty, wall-face tunnelling, unreachable TUNNEL instances, the lineage defects).

**Breadth-first fitness (run 7).** The search fitness becomes a weighted mean over engines (Kinetix reachable split + Craftax + Gymnax) with held-out worlds/seeds/achievements per engine and a held-out engine; `scripts/slurm/eval_server_multi.sbatch` + `scripts/launch_run7.sh` (armed behind the A40 fitness server).

**Accuracy across breadth** means: the *same frozen procedure* is evaluated on every suite; per-suite numbers are reported with 5-seed CIs and paired per-instance CIs against the strongest baseline; a suite where the procedure loses is reported as such.


**Structural feasibility (Aug 29 evening).** `scripts/structural_feasibility.py` checks whether a planted defect's witness can fire at all. 5 of 337 instances (all TUNNEL: the arm levels and h15_swing_up) are unwitnessable by construction — every dynamic body is joint-excluded from the de-solidified target, and `any_dynamic_inside` counts only collidable bodies. Four sit in the search split and are exactly four of its 24 never-reached instances; one is in held-out levels; none in the held-out (OOB) class. The TUNNEL injector now refuses such targets. SHORTCUT and DISARMED are contact witnesses and are *not* matrix-gated: jax2d generates contact manifolds geometrically and applies the collision matrix only when resolving impulses, so Kinetix's own reward fires for overlapping jointed pairs (six matrix-excluded SHORTCUT instances were in fact reached). Consequence for the ≥90%-of-reachable target: the reachable denominator already excluded these four; the honest statement of the remaining gap is 20 unreached-but-not-provably-infeasible search instances. **Hardness tiers (Aug 30):** the constant-input envelope probe (`--envelope 256`) splits witnessable targets into a *constant-input tier* (a single held action reaches them: 36% of search, 36% of held-out-level, 15% of sealed-class targets) and an *exploration tier*; `scripts/tier_table.py` reports detection per tier. On the fixed harness the best programs reach ~100% of the constant tier and 25–43% of the exploration tier — the exploration tier is the benchmark's real content.
## Chapter 3 — Methods: the discovery loop, its baselines, and its rivals

**The loop.** ShinkaEvolve proposes patches to the `act/restore/archive` hooks; fitness = search-split score; GPU fitness servers (A40 + H200) score candidates on the size-l split (3 seeds, 128×1024 steps, ~11 min). Runs: 1 (local fitness, OOB held out) → best 0.699; 2 (macrosweep seed, hard-weighted) 0.621 — its candidates evaluate at 0.606 / 0.587 / 0.334, well below run 1: the seed family matters more than the fitness weighting; 3 (ANOMALY held out) 0.478 from 0.354; 4 (GPU fitness, run-1 seed) **0.646 from 0.610** (gen 30; top-2 queued for held-out evaluation); 5 (movertarget seed) stopped at gen 21: 0.545 → 0.607; 6 (reachable-only split) deferred; **7 (multi-engine fitness)** starts tonight on the H200.

**Round-2 Opus designs (Aug 29, 5 seeds).** target-cycle 0.585 / 0.574 / 0.316 (36% of the unseen class); **pair-ladder 0.630 / 0.613 / 0.382 (46%)** — the strongest hand design so far, beating macrosweep (0.588 / 0.573 / 0.296) on every split and closing most of the gap to the evolved program (0.666 / 0.617 / 0.418, 47%): the remaining margin is +0.036 on search, +0.004 on held-out levels and +0.036 (1 pt of detection) on the unseen class. Consequence for the claims: "discovered > hand-designed" holds against every design a designer wrote *before* seeing the benchmark's diagnostics, but a designer given the same failure analysis the search had gets within noise on transfer. Honest framing: the search's contribution is finding the structure, and an informed designer can reproduce it once told what matters. The extremes-archive design (elites, 5 seeds) finished at 0.588 / 0.563 / 0.322 (35%): search-split parity with macrosweep, weaker on both transfer splits — per-body extremes alone do not transfer. Action: seed a search from pair-ladder (run 8) — seed family matters more than weighting (run 2), so a new family may lift the ceiling.

**Baselines the exploration literature expects.** random, sticky random, Go-Explore (seed), PPO + {count, RND, ICM, curiosity-proximity, task}; *to add:* CCPT-style curiosity ⊕ imitation (T6); coverage as a secondary metric — **implemented** in the harness (coarse-cell table; recorded by every evaluation from now on; a 1× re-evaluation of the key testers is queued).

**Rival discovery methods (same problem, equal budget).** One-shot expert design (Opus rounds 1–3: eleven programs; the round-3 agent's diagnostics found the TUNNEL infeasibility); no-LLM search (constant tuning; restarts; adaptive portfolio); *pilots passed (T5):* agentic loop (0.616 → 0.620 in 6 evaluations) and AIDE-style tree search (0.608 → 0.632 in 6) — all proposals valid; **full runs (30 evaluations each) started** on the Kinetix fitness, same seed as run 4. Every rival's best program gets the same held-out evaluation.

**Known weaknesses.** Fitness noise (±0.02 local, ±0.01 GPU) bounds every search; the gains after the first generations are inside it. The pool contains unreachable instances (constant zeros in the fitness). Programs depend on the Kinetix feature layout (a feature adapter is required for any other engine).

## Chapter 5 — Usefulness: what would convince a skeptic

1. **Solve what RL cannot.** On K-Goal, count the levels solved by the discovered procedure within budget that PPO (any objective) does not solve; Kinetix's own paper lists levels its RL agents fail. A discovered *procedure* solving them at a fraction of PPO's environment steps is a usefulness result an RL audience understands.
2. **Cost per target.** Simulator steps and wall-clock to first witness, per suite, vs each baseline — the number a studio or an RL practitioner would actually use. *First numbers (results/cost.json):* on the held-out class, median 5.2k steps for the discovered procedure vs 42k (PPO+count) and 24k (PPO+RND) on the 21 instances all six testers find; ~3.2 s per instance-seed on one A40.
3. **Cross-engine reach.** The same frozen procedure on Craftax achievements (C-Ach) — even a handful of hard achievements reached faster than PPO-RND would carry the generality claim.
4. **Real anomalies as validation, not headline.** The replay-verified list with severities; the upstream report to the Kinetix authors.
5. **Human anchor.** A small human-tester sample on ten planted instances (Ariyurek's template) — the single most credible number for a testing audience.
